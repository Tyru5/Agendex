# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Agendex serves solo AI-coding power users and engineering teams who need a reliable way to manage the plans their coding agents create. Users may be working locally on one machine, coordinating across multiple machines, or reviewing shared cloud plans with teammates. Their core job is to find, inspect, organize, share, and track agent plans without losing context as agent activity grows.

## Product Purpose

Agendex indexes plans and sessions produced by coding agents and turns them into a searchable, navigable workspace. The product exists so users can see what their agents planned, what changed, which work matters, and where collaboration or follow-up is needed. Success means users can move from scattered local plan files to confident plan review and coordination with minimal setup, low friction, and clear provenance.

## Brand Personality

Elegant, modern, concise. The interface should feel precise and capable, with enough polish to earn trust but enough restraint to stay useful during repeated daily work. Copy should be direct, economical, and action-oriented.

## Anti-references

Avoid cluttered project-management suites, toy AI dashboard visuals, bloated SaaS marketing patterns, excessive decorative chrome, and interfaces that make agent work feel more complicated than the underlying plans. Agendex should not look like a generic task board, a novelty AI wrapper, or a loud analytics product.

## Design Principles

1. Plan-first clarity: surface plan title, source, recency, status, and next actions before decorative or promotional material.
2. Local-to-cloud continuity: local OSS workflows and Cloud Pro collaboration should feel like one product with different capabilities, not separate apps.
3. Expert calm under volume: support dense scanning, filtering, and search while keeping visual hierarchy quiet and readable.
4. Collaboration without ceremony: sharing, comments, history, tags, and collections should help teams review plans without turning Agendex into heavyweight project management.
5. Trust through provenance: make file sources, sync state, daemon health, and permission boundaries understandable at the point of use.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Core workflows should be keyboard-friendly, readable in light and dark themes, and usable with reduced motion. Status, sync, and plan-state indicators should not rely on color alone. Motion should be purposeful, optional where appropriate, and limited to opacity and transform for comfort and performance.
